3. Email Marketing Best Practices

Email marketing continues to deliver the highest ROI of any digital marketing channel. Follow these best practices:

  • Personalization: Use customer data to personalize subject lines and content
  • Segmentation: Divide your list into targeted segments
  • Mobile Optimization: Ensure emails look great on mobile devices
  • A/B Testing: Test subject lines, content, and CTAs

4. SEO Fundamentals
